About

26awake keeps watch on the news so you don't have to refresh twenty tabs. Every article is rated by where it comes from: health authorities and wire services count, tabloids and anonymous channels don't, and a site repeating its own story isn't counted twice.

Case numbers only appear when an official body, or at least two independent trusted outlets, report them — and official denials are shown right next to them.

Everything is collected automatically, so it can be wrong. Follow the source links and official health guidance.
